muscles.glb
===========

This file is a derivative of the Open 3D Model of Human Anatomy.

Source:  https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel
Core source:
https://anatomytool.org/content/open3dmodel-muscles-thorax-abdomen-and-back-english-labels
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Created by the departments of anatomy of Leiden University Medical Center,
University Medical Center Utrecht, Maastricht University Medical Center and
University of Leuven (KULAK), with anatomists of Amsterdam University Medical
Center (VUmc), Radboud University Medical Center Nijmegen and University of
Gent. Initial grant from the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science.
The model builds on the earlier BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy models.

The March 2026 thorax, abdomen and back model was created by the Open3D
project, with 3D modelling by Eungyeol Lee and anatomical content by Andreas
Herrler, O. Paul Gobée, George J.R. Maat, Jan Kooloos and Marco C. DeRuiter.
Its web model was prepared by Daniël Jansma.

Changes made in this derivative
-------------------------------

- Kept only the muscles this product names. Core uses the source model's rectus
  abdominis, external and internal abdominal obliques, and transversus
  abdominis. Bursae, tendon sheaths, ligaments, nerves and vessels were removed.
- Removed all textures. The material is generated at runtime.
- Decimated the retained geometry with meshoptimizer to 23,840 triangles, so it
  loads on a cheap phone.
- Renamed nodes with a muscle-group prefix so the app can render a group.

Because the source is ShareAlike, this derived file is released under the same
licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. The build script is at app/scripts/build-anatomy.mjs.
